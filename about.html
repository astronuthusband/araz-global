About Araz Global

One company, built across industries.

Araz Global brings together engineering, facility services, travel, security and automotive services under a single, professional company.

Company Introduction

Who We Are

Araz Global is a multi-service company operating across civil engineering, public facility maintenance, security, travel, and automotive services.

Rather than specializing in a single industry, Araz Global is structured to provide clients with a diverse range of essential services under one company — from G3 civil engineering and public facility renovation to Umrah and Hajj travel arrangements, security services, and automotive solutions.

Vision

Our Vision

To be recognised as a dependable, multi-disciplinary company delivering professional services across the industries we operate in.

Mission

Our Mission

To provide clients with professional, reliable and client-focused solutions across civil engineering, facility services, travel, security and automotive services.
